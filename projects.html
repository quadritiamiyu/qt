Complete Excel · Power Query · PivotTables

Pharmacy sales vs procurement review

Compared nine months of sales and purchasing data year-on-year to identify margin movement, supplier concentration risk and stock-out patterns across more than 2,000 product lines.

Business question: Where is margin pressure coming from, and how concentrated is supplier risk?

Approach: Cleaned and structured sales + procurement data, built PivotTables and Power Query transformations, analysed year-on-year movements and concentration metrics.

Key results: Gross sales ₦148.7m (−2.1% YoY); net sales flat; gross margin improved to 32.7%; procurement −7.9%. Identified rational product stops vs unmet-demand risk.

Goal: Support better purchasing decisions and inventory control.
